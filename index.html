How it works

Each English letter is converted to its alphabet index (A = 0), then its 1-based position in the text is added. The result is taken modulo 26 to determine the encrypted letter. Finally, the entire text is reversed. Decryption undoes these steps.

Cᵢ = (Pᵢ + i) mod 26
